Sontag throwing shade on Walter Benjamin's montage work:  

*Though collecting quotations could be considered as merely an ironic mimetism - victimless collecting, as it were - this should not be taken to mean that Benjamin disapproved of, or did not indulge in, the real thing. For it was Benjamin's conviction that reality itself invited - and vindicated - the once heedless, inevitably destructive ministrations of the collector. In a world that is well on its way to becoming one vast quarry, the collector becomes someone engaged in a pious work of salvage.* 

Sontag, Susan (1973) *On Photography,* pp. 75-76.
