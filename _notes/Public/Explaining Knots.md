This is to explain my tag #knots that appears in here, as sometimes my categories are a bit too idiosyncratic for sharing. 

When I come across a concept that articulates something very specific or interesting, I tend to incorporate it and then assign it to all manner of things as I see it manifest in other places. In this way, theory becomes something I embody or enact even in my daily life. I suppose this is also a way of pattern matching. 

For me, "knots" refers specifically to [R.D. Laing's ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R._D._Laing)*Knots* (1970), a book of poetic explorations of the complexities of human relationships and the ways in which we get stuck in relation to each other. Creating, as Laing calls them, *impasses.* 

An example (1970, p.58): 

*If I don't know I don't know  
I think I know  
If I don't know I know  
I think I don't know*

The word play here is obviously very appealing. Since reading this book, I have pointed at every experience of stuckness (either mine, or a fictional character's) and declared *knots.* Hence, it has become its own category. 

You can read *Knots* (1970) on the Internet Archive [here](https://archive.org/details/knots00rdla ). 

Laing, R. D. 1970. *Knots.* Tavistock Publications Limited.
